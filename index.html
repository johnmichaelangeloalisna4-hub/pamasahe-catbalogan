Passenger Rights & Scam Avoidance

Proteksyon hit pasahero kontra ha panloloko ha pletehan.

🤝 1. Agree on the fare before boarding
Always clarify the fare before stepping onto a tricycle or habal-habal to avoid awkward disputes at destination.
📋 2. Ask to see the posted fare matrix
All PUVs and tricycles are required by law to prominently display their approved tariff matrix inside the vehicle.
🚫 3. No '3-for-1' passenger charging
A driver cannot charge 3 passengers' fare for 1 person on a shared trip. Solo rides are only charged as special trips if you hired the entire vehicle.
🔢 4. Note the Plate or Body Number
Check the local franchise sticker and body number painted on the side and rear of the vehicle before or during the ride.
📸 5. Take a photo if safe
A quick photo of the vehicle plate or body number serves as indisputable evidence for city traffic inspectors.
🎓 6. 20% Mandatory Discount
Students, Senior Citizens, and PWDs are entitled by Philippine law to a 20% discount on all days, including holidays and weekends.
